# What to Do Before Your Cabinet Installation Day

## Outline

1. Follow the written SELA scope and readiness instructions for the specific project.
2. Confirm cabinets arrived complete and visibly undamaged before scheduling when applicable.
3. Clear approved work and access areas; protect or move personal items.
4. Confirm removal status and remember that removed cabinets remain onsite for customer disposal unless a separate qualified service is arranged in writing.
5. Confirm appliance information and disclose visible utility or wall/floor concerns.
6. Coordinate countertops, plumbing, electrical, flooring, and other trades outside SELA's cabinet scope.
7. Keep children and pets safely outside the work area.
8. Know who to contact: the SELA owner remains the communication point.
