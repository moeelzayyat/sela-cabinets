## Ad Strategy Notes

- **Primary goal:** Generate qualified consultation bookings and estimate requests.
- **Key markets:** Detroit metro, especially design-conscious suburbs such as Troy, Royal Oak, Farmington Hills, Ann Arbor, and West Bloomfield.
- **Key messaging:** premium semi-custom cabinets, in-home measurement, cabinet samples, design guidance, professional installation, and a polished local experience.
- **Creative direction:** show finished kitchens, cabinet detail shots, homeowner testimonials, and the consultation process.
- **Seasonal peaks:** Spring and fall are strong renovation planning seasons.
